This Agency Platform Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Vailo (“Vailo”, “we”, “us”) and the hospitality agency or property-management company registering for or using the Vailo admin platform (“Agency”, “you”).

The Agency manages one or more properties on behalf of property owners or landlords. By creating an account, onboarding properties, inviting guests, or otherwise using Vailo, you agree to this Agreement. If you accept on behalf of a company, you represent that you have authority to bind that entity.

1. Services

Vailo provides a hosted platform that may include, as enabled for your subscription:

  • Multi-property admin access, owner CRM, and property allocation tools;
  • Digital guest portals, house guides, and reservation calendars per unit;
  • Guest invitations, pre-arrival check-in, encrypted ID handling, and guest CRM summaries;
  • Analytics, AI-assisted content tools, local gems, features, and excursion listings;
  • Team access for agency staff with role-based permissions.

2. Agency responsibilities

You agree to:

  • Manage only properties you are authorised to represent;
  • Ensure each property owner is informed that their property and guest data are processed via Vailo;
  • Maintain accurate owner, property, unit, and guest records;
  • Determine and document the data-protection roles between the Agency, property owners, and Vailo (see Section 3);
  • Provide guests with appropriate privacy information, including Vailo’s guest Privacy Policy where relevant;
  • Comply with hospitality, rental, guest-registration, and agency-contract laws in every jurisdiction where you operate;
  • Keep invitation credentials secure and use pre-arrival / ID data only for lawful check-in purposes;
  • Ensure your staff and subcontractors with platform access follow this Agreement.

3. Guest personal data — roles

Guest data processed for properties you manage is usually controlled by the property owner or by the Agency, depending on your contract with the owner and applicable law. You must establish who acts as data controller for each property and inform Vailo where required.

  • Vailo acts as a data processor when storing or transmitting guest data on your documented instructions through enabled platform features.
  • The Agency and/or property owner act as controller(s) for guest reservation, pre-arrival, portal, and CRM data.

You instruct Vailo to process guest data using subprocessors including Google Firebase, Resend, and Google Gemini/Firebase AI where enabled, and to apply platform retention rules (including deletion of pre-arrival identity data 7 days after checkout).

4. Property owners and third parties

You are responsible for your contractual relationship with property owners, including fees, content rights, and liability for property-specific guest communications. Vailo is not a party to your owner agreements unless explicitly stated in writing.

5. Pre-arrival, ID documents, and CRM

Where enabled, pre-arrival check-in may collect guest details and optional encrypted identity documents. Authorised Agency users may access submissions for properties they manage. Identity files are automatically deleted after the configured retention period following checkout. Guest profile summaries may persist in property CRM until deleted or the service ends.

6. Fees and payment

Fees and plan limits are as agreed in your order form or commercial terms. Non-payment may result in suspension across all properties under your agency account.

7. Acceptable use

You must not use Vailo unlawfully, access properties outside your mandate, resell the platform without written consent, or attempt to bypass security controls.

8. Intellectual property

Vailo retains platform IP. You and your property owners retain content you upload. You grant Vailo a licence to host and display that content to provide the Services.

9. Warranties, liability, and indemnity

The Services are provided “as is” to the extent permitted by law. Vailo’s aggregate liability is limited to fees paid by you in the twelve (12) months before the claim. You indemnify Vailo against claims arising from your agency operations, owner relationships, property content, or breach of this Agreement.

10. Term, changes, and governing law

This Agreement continues while you use the Services. Material changes may be notified via the admin platform. Governing law: Greece; courts of Athens, Greece, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.
