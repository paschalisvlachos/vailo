This Excursion Provider Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Vailo (“Vailo”, “we”, “us”) and the business listing excursions or experiences in the Vailo guest portal (“Provider”, “you”).

By registering as an excursion provider, publishing listings, or receiving guest booking requests through Vailo, you agree to this Agreement. If you accept on behalf of a company, you represent that you have authority to bind that entity.

1. Services

Vailo provides technology that may include:

  • Excursion and experience listings visible in property guest portals (subject to visibility rules);
  • Guest booking request forms capturing contact details, party size, date, and notes;
  • Provider admin tools to manage listings, availability, and booking records;
  • Attribution linking bookings to guest stays and properties where applicable.

Vailo is a technology platform, not the organiser or seller of your experiences. You remain responsible for delivering the service, pricing, payments, cancellations, insurance, and compliance.

2. Provider responsibilities

You agree to:

  • Provide accurate business, contact, licensing, and listing information;
  • Honour confirmed bookings and communicate promptly with guests;
  • Set clear prices, inclusions, exclusions, meeting points, and cancellation terms in your listings;
  • Collect and process guest payments directly unless another arrangement is explicitly agreed in writing;
  • Maintain required permits, insurance, and safety standards for your activities;
  • Respond to guest personal data in compliance with applicable data-protection law;
  • Not offer illegal, unsafe, or discriminatory experiences.

3. Guest personal data — roles

For guest data submitted through excursion booking forms (name, email, phone, party details, notes):

  • You are the data controller for fulfilling and managing the booking.
  • Vailo is the data processor when storing booking records and making them available in your provider portal.
  • Property hosts may see that a guest booked an experience linked to their stay where platform features allow.

You instruct Vailo to process booking data using subprocessors such as Google Firebase. Do not use guest data for unrelated marketing without a lawful basis and, where required, consent.

4. Listing content and visibility

You grant Vailo a non-exclusive licence to display your listings, images, and descriptions in guest portals and marketing materials related to the platform. Property hosts may control whether excursions appear to their guests via visibility settings.

5. Fees and commercial terms

Commission, subscription fees, or referral terms are as agreed in your provider onboarding or separate commercial agreement. Unless stated otherwise, Vailo does not collect payment from guests on your behalf.

6. Acceptable use

You must not publish misleading listings, infringe third-party rights, spam guests, or attempt to circumvent platform security or attribution.

7. Intellectual property

Vailo retains platform IP. You retain your listing content and grant Vailo a licence to host and display it as described above.

8. Warranties, liability, and indemnity

The platform is provided “as is”. Vailo does not guarantee booking volume or guest attendance. To the extent permitted by law, Vailo’s aggregate liability is limited to fees paid by you to Vailo in the twelve (12) months before the claim. You indemnify Vailo against claims arising from your experiences, guest injuries, misrepresentations, or breach of this Agreement.

9. Term and termination

Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days. You may stop using the provider portal at any time. Upon termination, listings may be removed and booking records handled per applicable law and your DPA with Vailo.

10. Changes and governing law

We may update this Agreement with notice where practicable. Governing law: Greece; courts of Athens, Greece, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.
