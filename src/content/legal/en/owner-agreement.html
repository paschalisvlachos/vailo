1. Services

Vailo provides a hosted software platform that may include, as enabled for your subscription:

  • Digital house guide and guest portal for your properties and units;
  • Reservation calendar with manual bookings and iCal import from booking channels;
  • Guest invitations (link and password), optional automatic invitation emails, and WhatsApp-oriented workflows;
  • Pre-arrival digital check-in (arrival details, guest count, contact data, house rules acceptance, optional ID upload, optional transfer upsell);
  • Encrypted storage of guest ID documents with automated deletion after checkout;
  • Admin tools to view pre-arrival data and export declaration documents;
  • Guest CRM summaries (e.g. house guests list, returning-guest indicators, guest profiles linked to stays);
  • Guest usage analytics and issue/escalation tools;
  • AI-assisted content and guest assistant features;
  • Local gems, features, excursions, and related marketplace or listing tools where available.

Features may change over time. Beta or preview features may be offered without warranty.

2. Customer responsibilities

  • Maintain the security of your admin login credentials.

3. Guest personal data — roles

For guest and visitor personal data processed through the platform in connection with your properties:

  • You are the data controller. You decide what guest data is collected and why (e.g. check-in, communication, registration).
  • Vailo is the data processor when we store, transmit, or otherwise process that data on your documented instructions via the platform features you enable.

You instruct us to process guest data as necessary to provide the Services, including storage in Google Firebase, sending emails via Resend, AI processing via Google Gemini/Firebase AI where enabled, and scheduled deletion of pre-arrival identity data according to platform retention settings (currently 7 days after checkout for pre-arrival submissions and encrypted ID files).

A separate Data Processing Agreement (DPA) or data-processing annex may be made available in the Legal Documents section of the admin platform or upon request at privacy@vailo.app.

4. Pre-arrival check-in and identity documents

Where you use pre-arrival check-in:

  • Guests may optionally upload identity documents (JPEG, PNG, WebP, or PDF, size limits apply);
  • Documents are encrypted at rest; direct client access to storage paths is blocked;
  • Authorised admins on your account may decrypt and download documents through secure server functions;
  • Pre-arrival fields and ID files are automatically purged after the configured retention period following checkout (unless deletion fails for technical reasons, in which case the system retries);
  • Guest contact details updated via check-in may remain on the booking record;
  • Guest profile summaries may be retained in your property CRM until you delete them or terminate the service.

You are solely responsible for determining whether ID collection is necessary and lawful for your jurisdiction and property type, and for using exported declaration PDFs in compliance with local registration rules.
