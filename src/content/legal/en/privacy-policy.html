Last updated: 11 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected and used when you access the Vailo guest portal (the “Portal”) for a holiday property or hotel unit. The Portal is operated by Vailo (“we”, “us”) on behalf of the property owner or manager hosting your stay (“Host”).

For most guest-stay data, the Host is the data controller and Vailo processes data on the Host’s instructions as a data processor. For platform operations (e.g. website contact forms, platform administration), Vailo may act as controller. Contact us at privacy@vailo.app for privacy questions. For requests relating specifically to your stay, you may also contact your Host directly.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Guests and visitors who open a property’s Portal (via invitation link, on-site access during an active stay, or an authorised visitor code);
  • Anyone who submits a pre-arrival check-in form linked to a reservation;
  • Anyone who books an excursion or experience offered through the Portal;
  • Anyone who uses AI assistant or concierge features inside the Portal.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Reservation and portal access

Depending on how you access the Portal, we may process:

  • Your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and preferred language;
  • Stay dates and unit/property name;
  • Invitation credentials (access links and passwords are generated for your stay; passwords are stored in hashed form on our servers);
  • Portal session identifiers stored in your browser until your access period ends (typically until two days after checkout);
  • How you accessed the Portal (invitation, on-site during stay, visitor code, or admin preview).

2.2 Pre-arrival check-in

If your Host uses digital pre-arrival check-in, you may provide:

  • Expected arrival time and number of guests;
  • Contact phone and optional email;
  • Optional date of birth;
  • Special requests (e.g. early check-in, crib, transfer);
  • Confirmation that you accept the property house rules;
  • Optional identity document (photo or PDF of passport/ID), if requested by the Host.

Identity documents are encrypted before storage. They are used only for lawful check-in and guest-registration requirements and are automatically deleted 7 days after checkout, together with other pre-arrival submission details. Contact details updated during check-in may remain on the reservation record for the Host.

If the Host offers an optional transfer (e.g. from port or airport), you may request it during check-in. Payment for such transfers is arranged directly with the Host (cash on arrival unless otherwise stated); Vailo does not process payments for transfers.

2.3 Portal usage and analytics

To understand how the Portal is used and to improve the service, we collect first-party usage data such as:

  • Pages or sections opened (house guide, local gems, excursions, assistant);
  • Device type and operating system (derived from your browser);
  • Anonymous visitor identifiers if you browse without a booking session;
  • Aggregated interaction counts linked to your stay when you are logged in as a guest.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies in the guest Portal. Admin preview and authorised tester sessions are excluded from guest analytics.

2.4 AI assistant and issue reporting

If you use the in-portal AI assistant:

  • We ask for your consent before processing your messages;
  • Your questions, optional images you attach, and AI responses may be processed by Google Gemini via Firebase AI to generate replies;
  • Truncated message text may be stored in usage analytics;
  • If you ask us to escalate a question to the Host, we send your question and the AI reply to the Host by email and store an issue record for the property.

AI responses are informational only and are not professional, legal, medical, or emergency advice.

2.5 Excursion and experience bookings

If you book an excursion through the Portal, we collect contact and booking details (name, email, phone, date, party size, notes, pricing) and link them to the excursion provider and, where applicable, to your property stay. The excursion provider is responsible for fulfilling the booking.

2.6 Technical and security data

We use Google Firebase and related infrastructure. In production, Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA Enterprise) may process technical signals to protect against abuse. Server logs may contain IP addresses and request metadata for security and troubleshooting.

3. Why we use your data

  • Provide the Portal — give you access to stay information, guides, and Host communications (performance of arrangements for your stay / legitimate interests).
  • Pre-arrival and registration — help the Host prepare for arrival and meet applicable registration obligations (legal obligation and/or legitimate interests of the Host).
  • Communications — send invitation emails, optional post-stay thank-you messages, and Host escalations where enabled (legitimate interests / performance of stay arrangements).
  • Improve and secure the service — analytics, fraud prevention, and platform reliability (legitimate interests).
  • AI features — only with your consent where required.

4. Who we share data with

  • Your Host — reservation details, pre-arrival submissions, guest issues, and CRM summaries visible in their admin account.
  • Excursion providers — booking details for experiences you request.
  • Service providers (sub-processors) including:
    • Google Firebase / Google Cloud (hosting, database, storage, functions, AI);
    • Resend (transactional email delivery);
    • Google Places and mapping services where location features are used;
    • OpenStreetMap Nominatim for geocoding in concierge features.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Retention

  • Pre-arrival data and ID documents: deleted automatically within 7 days after checkout (identity files are removed from encrypted storage).
  • Portal browser session: removed when your access period expires or you clear site data.
  • Reservation contact fields: retained by the Host for as long as they manage the booking and applicable records.
  • Guest CRM profiles: retained by the Host property until deleted by the Host or under Host’s retention practices.
  • Analytics and issue records: retained for operational and improvement purposes unless deleted earlier by platform or Host policies.

6. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process data in the European Union and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or provider certifications. You may contact us for more information about transfers relevant to your stay.

7. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, UK, or another jurisdiction with similar laws, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability where applicable. You may also withdraw consent (without affecting prior lawful processing) and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

To exercise rights relating to your stay, contact your Host first. You may also email privacy@vailo.app and we will assist or redirect your request to the appropriate controller.

8. Children

The Portal is not directed at children under 16. Pre-arrival forms collect information about the guest party but should not be used to collect children’s identity documents unless required by applicable law and authorised by the Host.

9. Security

We use encryption for identity documents, access controls for admin functions, hashed invitation passwords, and cloud security measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please protect your invitation password and notify your Host if you suspect misuse.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Material changes may be communicated through the Portal or by your Host where appropriate.
